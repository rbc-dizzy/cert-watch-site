Privacy policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

1. About CERT-WATCH

CERT-WATCH is a privately operated monitoring tool used by its owner to monitor TLS certificates, certificate-renewal activity, and selected service health on servers and infrastructure that the owner administers. It is not offered as a public service and has no public user registration.

2. Information processed by the monitoring tool

CERT-WATCH processes technical monitoring information such as host and service identifiers, domain names, certificate fingerprints and expiry dates, certificate/key match results, TLS check results, selected service states, renewal timer/job observations, incident history, and diagnostic reports. This information is collected from systems administered by the owner.

Monitoring reports, history, and incident state are stored locally on the owner's privately administered NAS. They are subject to local retention and storage limits configured by the owner. The monitoring tool is not designed to sell this information or provide it to data brokers.

3. Google user data and Gmail API

Email notifications are planned but are not currently enabled. If the owner enables them in the future, CERT-WATCH will use the Google Gmail API with the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send OAuth scope only to send monitoring alert and recovery emails on the owner's behalf to the recipient address configured by the owner.

CERT-WATCH will not use the Gmail API to read, search, list, modify, or delete messages, access the inbox, or inspect message content. Google user data will not be used for advertising, profiling, sale, or unrelated purposes.

Emails may contain the relevant alert details, such as a monitored host or domain name, the type and time of an incident, certificate expiry information, selected service status, and recovery information. Google processes and delivers those messages as the email service provider. Sent messages may remain in the owner's Gmail Sent folder under Google's own service and retention settings.

4. OAuth credentials and storage

If Gmail integration is enabled, the OAuth client credentials and refresh token will be stored on the owner's private NAS with filesystem permissions restricted to the system administrator. Access tokens will be used to call the Gmail API for the limited sending purpose described above. The owner can revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings.

5. Sharing and disclosure

CERT-WATCH does not sell, rent, or share monitoring information with advertisers or data brokers. The information included in an alert email is transmitted to Google for delivery to the recipient configured by the owner. The hosting provider for this public information page may process ordinary technical request data in accordance with its own privacy practices.

6. Retention and deletion

Monitoring reports, observations, and incident records are retained locally according to the tool's configured retention rules and storage limits. OAuth credentials are retained while email notifications are configured and may be removed by the system administrator. Emails already sent are subject to the owner's Gmail account settings and retention controls.

7. Security

The tool is intended for private use on infrastructure administered by its owner. Local access to stored monitoring data and OAuth credentials is restricted by system permissions. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the way CERT-WATCH accesses or uses Google user data materially changes. The effective date above will be revised when changes are made.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy or CERT-WATCH, use the application support email displayed on the CERT-WATCH Google OAuth consent screen.
